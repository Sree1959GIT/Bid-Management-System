# BOI Repository Manifest

This repository is the controlled implementation home for Business Opportunity Intelligence (BOI).

## Specifications
- `specs/BOI-PRD.md` - product requirements and Phase 1 boundaries
- `specs/ARCHITECTURE.md` - thin core, profiles, Skills, Workflows, Agents and connectors
- `specs/KNOWLEDGE-MODEL.md` - projects, capabilities, tenders, outcomes, evidence and provenance
- `specs/SKILL-MODEL.md` - reusable Skill contract and generic opportunity scout
- `specs/WORKFLOW-MODEL.md` - common lifecycle with business-line-specific stages
- `specs/SECURITY-MODEL.md` - secrets, data handling and external-access rules
- `specs/PHASE-PLAN.md` - phased implementation roadmap

## Codex prompts
- `prompts/CODEX-MASTER-PROMPT.md` - overall implementation charter
- `prompts/CODEX-OPERATING-PROTOCOL.md` - ongoing development discipline
- `prompts/CODEX-PHASE-1-INGESTION.md` - incremental document ingestion
- `prompts/CODEX-PHASE-2-KNOWLEDGE.md` - evidence-aware knowledge layer
- `prompts/CODEX-PHASE-3-PROFILE.md` - Electronics & Automation business profile
- `prompts/CODEX-PHASE-4-GEM.md` - GeM connector validation
- `prompts/CODEX-PHASE-5-OPPORTUNITY-SCOUT.md` - intent-driven high-precision opportunity scouting

## Working rule
Read the relevant specification before changing the implementation. Work in small validated increments and preserve provenance.
